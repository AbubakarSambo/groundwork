Q4 priorities, draft from the leadership offsite. Pasting as is, I will tidy later. Written by Bisola Ade (CEO).

PRIORITY 1: Grow revenue to N520m for the quarter, from N410m last quarter. Owner: Amina Yusuf, Sales. Measure: booked revenue. Target N520m. Also new dealers signed, at least 25 (last quarter we signed 18).

PRIORITY 2: Keep customers. Owner: Tobi Lawal, Customer Support. Measure: customer complaints per 100 orders, must stay under 3 (it is 4.2 now). Response time under 2 hours. Target 2 hours.

PRIORITY 3: Cut waste in operations. Owner: Ibrahim Danjuma, Operations. Measure: delivery cost as a share of sales, from 11% to under 9%. Late deliveries under 5% (now 12%).

PRIORITY 4: Cash. Owner: Funmi Ojo, Finance. Measure: receivables over 60 days under N30m (now N52m). Credit as a share of sales under 35%. Close the books within 5 working days each month.

PRIORITY 5: Hire and keep good people. Owner: Hannah Cole, People. 6 hires by end of November, turnover under 4%. Everyone has an agreed 30 60 90 or quarterly plan by 31 October.

Strategy: we win on reliability, not on price. Expansion in existing regions before new regions.

How we work: every priority has one owner, owners report on Fridays, a measure nobody can check does not count.

Dates: Q4 ends 31 December. Hiring plan by 15 October. Price letter to customers by 10 November.
