SUPPLY AGREEMENT — NORTHWIND AND BELLAMY LTD (extract)

3. TERM
3.1 This agreement runs for twelve months from 1 October and renews automatically
    unless either party gives sixty days' written notice.

4. SERVICE LEVELS
4.1 Bellamy will respond to a priority incident within four working hours.
4.2 Monthly uptime will not fall below 99.5%.
4.3 Where 4.2 is missed in two consecutive months, Northwind may terminate on
    thirty days' notice without penalty.

7. CHARGES
7.1 Charges are fixed for the first twelve months.
7.2 Any increase after that is capped at CPI plus two per cent.

9. DATA
9.1 Bellamy processes Northwind data only on written instruction.
9.2 On termination Bellamy returns or deletes all Northwind data within thirty days.
