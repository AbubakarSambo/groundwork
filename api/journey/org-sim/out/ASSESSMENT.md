# Groundwork — eighteen grounds, one organisation

One org (Meridian Health Group), Sahar the only constant, twelve people with
stable levels and styles across grounds. Run strictly in order against the live
model through the real API: sessions within a ground concurrent, the grounds
themselves never. Grounds 1-10 on the free tier, 11-18 after subscribing.

Counts are measured from the record. Ratings and "what shifted" are judgements,
marked as such, and kept apart from the counts on purpose.

**Most common blocker:** none of the grounds was blocked outright. The recurring

**Single highest-priority fix:** the engine names the person it is talking to as
the other party — "this ground is for you and Eric", said to Eric — fifteen times
across six grounds, in the opening sentence of a check-in. It is small, cheap to
fix, and it makes the product look like it does not know who is in the room, on
exactly the grounds where trust matters most.
