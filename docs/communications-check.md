# Communications check, wip/release (10 October 2026)

Done on test bed copy a (Msingi Foods and the other four organisations), with the mail catcher and the signed WhatsApp recorder, and the clock moved where something is scheduled. Nothing was sent to anybody: mail goes to the catcher, WhatsApp to the recorder, and no live key is read. The scripts are in `api/journey/`: `every-email-as-it-arrives.ts`, `sign-up-and-sign-in-flows.ts`, `engine-journeys-and-breaking-it.ts` and `role-privacy-sweep.ts`; the clock was moved with `bed.sh clock advance --to 2026-10-11T20:00:00Z --fast`.
