`OKR_ALIGNMENT` promised "set goals, targets or OKRs for your team... you can see what is on track".
The pack said "MOMENT: OKR alignment across teams. PURPOSE: Each person submits their own OKRs and
shows how they connect to the company OKRs... before the planning cycle locks in." The conversation
was not malfunctioning. It was faithfully running the wrong scenario.

### The rule I'd give Claude

The **first check-in should establish the person's picture**, not interrogate them.

The **second should pick up the thread**: *"Last time you said X. What's happened since?"*

The **last should close the loop**: *"Where did we get to?"*

And across all of them, Groundwork is trying to understand:

**What were you meant to do? → What is actually happening? → How do you see it? → What has changed? → Where do we need to act or support?**

That gives the conversations a common spine without making every scenario feel the same.
