B-041: L1/A1, /start, the entry set-up chat, refreshing while Groundwork is still composing its
reply. Admin (fresh test organisation), browser, 1440 width. Reproduced with Playwright
(e2e/sweep/situations-flows.spec.ts, "refresh halfway through set-up").

Steps:
1. On /start, typed "We run a small ops team and want to keep a weekly read on the work." and
   pressed Send. While the page still showed "Thinking..." (the reply had not arrived yet),
   refreshed the page.
2. After the refresh, the page is back to the very first, blank "Set up a check-in" screen
   ("Describe it in your own words. Nothing is sent to anyone until you press send.", an empty
   textbox, no history) - as if nothing had been said. Screenshots:
   _situations-refresh-before-1440.png (mid "Thinking...") and
   _situations-refresh-after-1440.png (blank start screen).

This is a different page from the one M2 already covers (the participant's check-in chat, which
resumes correctly after a refresh/navigate-away - verified separately and not a bug). Here, the
one thing the lead typed is gone with no warning, and they must describe their situation again
from scratch.

Severity: Confusing, arguably Stops someone using it if it happens more than once in a row (nothing
in the rules promises this chat survives a refresh, but losing a freshly-typed answer with no
"are you sure" or recovery is the same class of problem the M2 "nothing lost" requirement exists
to prevent, just on a different screen - engine or client state, not determined which here).
