## Across all the rows

- **Pages nobody can reach:** none confirmed outright, but three are worth a second look by whoever owns their links. Row 05 (`/join`) has no in-app link or email template pointing to it that this pass could find - join mail now goes through `/join/confirm` (row 64) instead, so this may be a leftover path. Row 63 (`/start/team`) and row 70 (`/preview/reports`) also have no incoming link found anywhere in the client or the mail templates; row 70 is an internal staff tool so that may be by design, but row 63 reads like a page meant to be shared (a second landing page, pitched at a team rather than a person), and is worth confirming with whoever sends that link.
- **Pages with no way out:** row 08 (Pricing), when a signed-in admin reaches it, drops the entire app side menu and leaves only the browser's own back button to return to the product - every other page in the audit keeps at least one link back into the app.
- **Two pages doing the same job:** rows 08 (Pricing) and 39 (Plan and billing) show the identical five paid tiers at the same prices, once as the public marketing page and once inside the app with usage figures beside them - not wrong, since one is for someone not yet signed in, but a signed-in admin can reach both and sees the same cards twice. Rows 61 (Set password) and 62 (Reset password) are, correctly, one shared component doing two related jobs rather than a duplication to fix. Row 34 (`/grounds/:id/report`) is a deliberate redirect onto row 32/38's own tabs, not a second page competing with them.
- **Pages that make sense to one role but not another:** row 11 (You) makes full sense for a lead, team member or guest, who each have reports of their own, but prints an empty "Your board" heading for an admin, who typically has none (see B-043). Row 42 (More) was only exercised for a team member in this pass; it is phone-only navigation that every role would reach at that width, so it should be checked for admin, lead and guest too. Row 65 (Waiting for an admin) makes sense only for someone newly joined and not yet let in - tested here as an existing admin, it never shows the state it exists for.
- **Pages that look different from the prototype:** a full side-by-side against every file in `docs/prototype/screens/` was not done this pass (see the report for what was and wasn't covered); the spot checks made - the sign-in screen, Today, Company and the Suites switchboard - matched the prototype's layout, type and colour closely, including the admin's onboarding card being the one addition a brand-new organisation's Today page has that the prototype's more established example does not. Two smaller inconsistencies inside the product itself, not against the prototype, are worth a look: row 64's refusal screen uses a different, smaller logo treatment than every other refusal page in this audit (rows 02, 04, 05, 06, 49, 56, 58-62, 69 all centre a card with the normal inline wordmark); and the same "This part is for Groundwork staff" refusal renders with the full app sidebar on row 70 but bare, with no sidebar at all, on rows 49-53 and 73.
