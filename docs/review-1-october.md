# Review, 1 October: what people were actually served

Reviewer's read of the stored scenario runs built with tonight's work:

- `api/journey/all-24/re-a/{sales,seniorhire,loans,realign}.json` (latest build; sales has 3 sessions, seniorhire 4, loans and realign 1).
- `api/journey/all-24/run-21/*.json` for the other twenty scenarios (earlier build tonight, 1 session each).

For each session I read what the lead was served (`leadReport`, `board`), what the one recorded participant was served (`participantReport`, `participantBoard`), every turn of every conversation, and the stored `patterns`. Admin and colleague views are not stored in these files; where a line is on the shared board it is what a colleague in the same check-in would read, and I say so. Nothing here is judged from code. Every quote below is copied from the files.

Severity: **Critical** (privacy, a label or a false fact about a person, or a page that tells the reader the wrong thing), **High** (a person would be confused or misled, or a rule in CLAUDE.md or `docs/decisions.md` is plainly broken), **Medium** (clearly wrong wording or a contradiction a careful reader would notice), **Low** (polish).

Verdict: **send back.** The judgement is right in about half the scenarios, but the pages around it still break the privacy rules in the evaluation check-ins, state invented facts about named people, speak about the lead in the third person to the lead, and contradict themselves on the same page.
